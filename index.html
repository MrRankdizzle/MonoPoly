    Learning targets

    • I can explain how monomers bond into polymers by dehydration synthesis, which releases a water molecule for every bond.
    • I can explain how polymers break back into monomers by hydrolysis, which uses up a water molecule for every bond.
    • I can identify the monomer, polymer, bond, and main job of carbohydrates, proteins, lipids, and nucleic acids.
